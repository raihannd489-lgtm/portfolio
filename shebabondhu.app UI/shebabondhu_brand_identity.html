Design principles
◻
Calm Minimalism
Lots of white space, one action per screen. Reduces cognitive load for first-time smartphone users.
◉
Touch-First
44px minimum tap targets, bottom-navigation bar, thumb-zone optimized layouts for one-handed use.
△
Trust Signals
Verified badges, star ratings, worker photos, and payment confirmation receipts reduce anxiety for new users.
⬡
Low-Data Friendly
Progressive image loading, skeleton screens, and offline-capable forms for patchy 3G connections.
Logo concept — symbol breakdown
House outline
Simple rooftop silhouette = home. The open walls (no fill) signal accessibility and welcome — not a closed fortress.
Two dots at roof edge
Green dot (worker) meets amber dot (customer) at the roofline — the moment of connection the platform enables.
Dashed arc
The dotted bridge between worker and customer — invisible until they connect, like a digital handshake across distance.
Wrench in doorway
A subtle tool mark inside the door anchors the platform in skilled craftsmanship — mason, electrician, painter.
